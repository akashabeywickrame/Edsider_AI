How it works

Three simple steps from a finished paper to one students can practise with.

STEP 1

Upload a paper

Add a typed or scanned question paper. Its questions are read and laid out ready to check.

STEP 2

Spot repeats

Questions that are already in use - even when they are worded differently - are pointed out, with a clear reason.

STEP 3

Review and publish

A teacher reviews every question before the paper reaches students. Nothing is changed without a person deciding.

Who it is for

One sign-in, three roles. Each person sees only the tools for their part of the work.

Upload papers, correct the questions, add any diagrams or pictures, and make sure nothing is repeated before it goes for review.
